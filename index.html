Organisers

Host

PlayStation Studios Creative, part of Sony Interactive Entertainment, is an award-winning internal agency serving the creative needs of PlayStation Studios worldwide. Working as a global team, we foster an environment where creativity can thrive while delivering industry-leading work. Across four core disciplines — Brand Design, UI/UX, Motion Graphics, and Film & Video — we partner closely with game development, marketing, and PR teams to create impactful projects for audiences around the world.

PlayStation Studios Malaysia, established in 2020, supports this broader mission as part of the global PlayStation Studios network, with the Kuala Lumpur-based Creative team contributing high-quality design and creative services across APAC and international markets.

Host

LEVEL UP KL is a premier Southeast Asia Game Developers Conference, organised by Malaysia Digital Economy Corporation (MDEC).

Over the past decade, LEVEL UP KL has grown into a flagship event for game developers across Southeast Asia to interact, learn and share knowledge—offering business and technical insights, as well as future-shaping trends from thought-leader keynotes and high-level panels from all over the world.

https://www.mdec.my/levelupkl

Celia Hodent

Summit Chair

Celia Hodent is recognized as a leader in the application of user experience and psychology in video game design and in the development of UX strategy and process in game studios.

She is also the founder and curator of the Game UX Summit, which launched in Durham, NC, in May of 2016, hosted by Epic Games.
